Investor green card (EB-5): new USCIS fees start November 30, 2026

USCIS raises EB-5 fees (I-526, I-526E, I-829, I-956) starting Nov. 30, 2026. What changes, who it affects, and what to do before you file.

USCIS published a final rule that changes fees for the EB-5 program, the investor green card. The rule appeared in the Federal Register on September 30, 2026, and takes effect on November 30, 2026.

The rule is clear: any application or petition postmarked on or after November 30, 2026, must pay the new fees.

Which fees change

Based on the rule's fee table as summarized by Envoy Global (always confirm on the official form page):

  • I-526 (standalone investor): from $3,675 to $7,615.
  • I-526E (regional center investor): from $3,675 to $7,850.
  • I-829 (remove conditions on residence): from $3,750 to $5,000.
  • I-956 (regional center designation): from $17,795 to $44,115.
  • I-956F (project or enterprise): from $17,795 to $42,675.
  • Some fees go down: the I-956 amendment and the I-956G annual statement.

A new form and other charges

  • New Form I-527 (amendment to a legacy I-526), with a $10,330 fee. It is for certain pre-2022-reform investors whose regional center was terminated or whose project was debarred.
  • A $75 technology fee applies to Form I-526 and initial Form I-526E filings.
  • Integrity Fund fees rise about 10%: from $1,000 to $1,100 for the I-526E, and from $10,000 to $11,000 (or $20,000 to $22,000) for regional centers.

Who it affects

  • Investors filing an I-526, I-526E, or I-829.
  • EB-5 regional centers and projects.
  • It does not change fees for other filings, such as work permits or citizenship.

What to do

  • If you were about to file, ask your attorney whether to send it before November 30, 2026. The postmark date counts.
  • Before paying, confirm the amount on the official form page or the USCIS fee calculator. A wrong payment can get your package rejected.
  • If your case is already pending, this rule does not make you pay again.
  • Track your case with your receipt number. CaseAlerta alerts you when the status changes.
