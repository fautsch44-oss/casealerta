USCIS finalizes the end of the 540-day automatic EAD extension

If you filed your work permit renewal (I-765) on or after October 30, 2025, your EAD no longer extends automatically. What changed and what to do.

On October 14, 2026 the Federal Register published a final rule adopting, without changes, the interim rule on employment authorization documents (EAD, Form I-766). It took effect the same day.

What changed

  • The up-to-540-day automatic extension only applies to renewals filed BEFORE October 30, 2025.
  • If you filed your renewal on or after October 30, 2025, your EAD expires on the printed date unless a law, regulation or Federal Register notice says otherwise.
  • Extensions tied to TPS (Temporary Protected Status) and others provided by law or notice are not affected.

What to do

  • Check the expiration date on your EAD card today.
  • File your renewal as early as possible: USCIS allows filing up to 180 days before expiration.
  • Track your renewal case so you know the moment it is approved. CaseAlerta alerts you by push and email.
  • If your job depends on your EAD, talk to your employer and, if in doubt, an immigration attorney.
