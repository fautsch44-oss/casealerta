"Case Is Being Actively Reviewed": what this USCIS status means and what to do

Your USCIS status says Case Is Being Actively Reviewed. What it means, why it can repeat, how long it may take, and what to do while you wait.

If your USCIS online status says "Case Is Being Actively Reviewed By USCIS", your case is under review inside USCIS.

This is an in-between status. It does not mean approval or denial. It only shows your case is moving through the process.

What it means in practice

  • USCIS is working on your case: it may be reviewing documents, background checks, or the evidence you sent.
  • It may appear after "Case Was Received", after your biometrics, or after you answered a request for evidence (RFE).
  • It is normal for this message to appear more than once, or for the date to update with no other change. That does not signal a problem.

How long it can take

  • There is no fixed date. It depends on the form, the office, and the workload.
  • USCIS publishes processing times by form and office. They are ranges based on recent cases and are updated monthly.
  • With your receipt date, the official tool tells you when you can contact USCIS if your case is taking longer than normal.

What may come next

  • A decision (for example "Case Was Approved").
  • A request for evidence (RFE) or an interview notice.
  • Other steps, such as your case being transferred to another office.

What to do

  • You do not need to do anything unless USCIS asks you for something.
  • Watch your mail and your USCIS online account. If an RFE arrives, answer it before the deadline it lists.
  • Keep your address up to date with USCIS (online or with Form AR-11).
  • Turn on CaseAlerta alerts to hear about the next change right away.
  • If your case is past the normal processing time, you can send an inquiry to USCIS through its Contact Center.
