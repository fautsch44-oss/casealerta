DV-2027 green card lottery still closed after an appeals court pause

DV-2027 green card lottery registration did not open on November 1. An appeals court paused the order. What happened and how to avoid scams.

The Diversity Visa (DV) program, known as the green card lottery, is still not open for DV-2027.

The State Department says it is changing the entry process and will announce the start date as soon as practicable. As of today, it has not given a date.

What happened in court

  • On October 14, 2026, a federal court in San Diego ordered the State Department to open registration on November 1.
  • On October 29, 2026, the Ninth Circuit Court of Appeals granted the government's request and paused that order while the appeal continues.
  • That is why registration did not open on November 1.
  • The court will set a briefing schedule later. No hearing date has been set.

Who this affects

  • People from eligible countries who planned to enter DV-2027.
  • It does not change other cases: family petitions, employment green cards, work permits and citizenship keep moving as usual.

Watch out for scams

  • Entry is free and only happens on the official State Department site: dvprogram.state.gov.
  • No one can enter you in DV-2027 today. If someone charges you to do it, it is a scam.
  • The government does not email or text you to say you won.

What to do

  • Do not pay anyone who promises to enter you in DV-2027.
  • Check only official State Department pages for the registration date.
  • Keep your details and a photo that meets the rules ready, so you can enter fast when it opens.
  • If you already have a USCIS case, add your receipt number to CaseAlerta to know the moment it changes.

This is general information, not legal advice.
