USCIS Form I-797C (Notice of Action): what it means and what to do when you get one

Form I-797C is USCIS's notice for receipts, rejections, transfers and appointments. It is not an approval. Learn to read it and what to do.

Form I-797C, Notice of Action, is a letter USCIS mails you to tell you something about your request.

What USCIS uses it for

  • Receipt: confirms USCIS received your request.
  • Rejection: your package was not accepted (for example, missing fee or signature).
  • Transfer: your case moved to another office.
  • Reopening: USCIS reopened your case.
  • Appointments: fingerprints or biometrics, interview, or a rescheduled appointment.

What Form I-797C is NOT

  • It is not an approval. USCIS says it is "only a receipt" proving you submitted your request.
  • It does not give you status or a benefit. USCIS has not yet decided if you qualify.
  • Some state, local, public or private agencies may accept it as supporting evidence for their own benefits.

How to read it fast

  • Look at the top for the notice type (for example, "Receipt Notice" or "Appointment Notice").
  • Copy your receipt number: 3 letters and 10 digits. You use it to track your case.
  • If it is an appointment, check the date, time and center address.
  • If it is a rejection, read the reason before you file again.

What to do

  • Read and follow the instructions. USCIS warns that ignoring them might delay your case.
  • Keep the original in a safe place and a digital copy. Older notices on sealed bond paper remain valid too.
  • Add your receipt number to CaseAlerta to get an alert when your status changes.
  • If you move, update your address with USCIS so you do not miss the next notice.
  • To see how long your case may take, check USCIS's official processing times. They are ranges, not exact dates.
