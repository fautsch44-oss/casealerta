I got an RFE from USCIS: what it is and what to do, step by step

A Request for Evidence (RFE) is not a denial. It is USCIS asking for more documents. How to answer well and on time.

If your status says "Request for Additional Evidence Was Sent", USCIS will mail you a letter called an RFE. It is not a denial: they need more proof to decide.

Step by step

  • Wait for the paper letter and read it fully. It lists exactly what they need and the deadline.
  • List each item and gather one document for each.
  • Put the RFE page on top of your package, as the letter asks.
  • Send EVERYTHING in one package, before the deadline, to the address in the letter.
  • Keep copies of everything and a tracked mailing receipt.

After you send it

  • When USCIS receives it, your status will change to "Response To USCIS' Request For Evidence Was Received".
  • If the RFE is complex, talk to an immigration attorney before answering.
