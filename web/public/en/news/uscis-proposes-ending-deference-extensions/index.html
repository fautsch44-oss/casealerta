USCIS proposes ending deference to prior approvals for work visa extensions

Proposed rule of October 14, 2026: extensions (H-1B, L-1 and others) would be reviewed from scratch. Comments due November 13, 2026.

On October 14, 2026 USCIS published a proposed rule that would remove 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5). Today that policy lets officers give weight to a prior approval when the facts have not changed.

What it would mean

  • Every extension of a work petition or stay (for example H-1B or L-1) would be reviewed as if it were new.
  • The petitioner or applicant must prove eligibility again in full.
  • It is a proposal: it is not in effect yet.

What to do

  • Employers: prepare complete extension packages, as if filing for the first time.
  • Anyone can comment at regulations.gov until November 13, 2026.
  • If your extension is pending, track it with CaseAlerta so you can answer fast if USCIS sends a Request for Evidence (RFE).
