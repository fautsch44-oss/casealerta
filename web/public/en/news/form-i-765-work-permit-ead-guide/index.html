Form I-765 (work permit): who files it, how to renew and what to expect

A simple guide to Form I-765 to apply for or renew a work permit (EAD): what it is, when to renew and how to track your USCIS case.

Form I-765, Application for Employment Authorization, is the form you use to ask USCIS for a work permit.

If USCIS approves it, you get a card called an EAD (Employment Authorization Document, Form I-766). The card shows its expiration date.

Who files it

  • People whose status already allows work do not need an EAD. For example, permanent residents use their green card.
  • Others must apply. For example: people with a pending I-485 (adjustment of status), eligible asylum applicants, TPS beneficiaries and F-1 students applying for OPT.
  • Each case uses an eligibility category, a code such as (c)(9) or (a)(12). The official I-765 instructions list them all.

How to file

  • Use the current form edition and pay the fee for your category. Some categories pay no fee or can ask for a waiver. Check everything on the official I-765 page.
  • Depending on your category, you file online or by mail. The official page shows the option and the correct address.
  • Sign the form and include the evidence the instructions ask for, such as a copy of your previous EAD or your receipt notice.

Renewal

  • USCIS lets you file a renewal up to 180 days before your EAD expires.
  • Automatic extension rules changed in 2026. For many new renewals, the card ends on its printed date. That is why filing early helps.
  • TPS-related extensions depend on the Federal Register notices for your country.

What happens next

  • You get a receipt notice (I-797C) with your case number.
  • You may be scheduled for biometrics (fingerprints and photo).
  • USCIS approves, asks for more evidence (RFE) or denies. If approved, it produces and mails the card.
  • Timing depends on the category and the office. USCIS publishes ranges on its processing times page. There is no exact decision date.

What to do

  • Check your EAD expiration date today and note the day you can renew (180 days before).
  • Read the official I-765 page and instructions before you fill it out.
  • When your receipt notice arrives, add the number to CaseAlerta to know when your status changes.
  • Keep your address up to date with USCIS so the card reaches your home.

This is general information, not legal advice. For your case, talk to an immigration attorney or an accredited representative.
