October 2026 Visa Bulletin: EB-2 moves forward strongly for Rest of World

For employment-based adjustment, USCIS uses the Dates for Filing chart. EB-2 Rest of World: March 15, 2026. EB-1 Rest of World: current.

The October 2026 Visa Bulletin opens fiscal year 2027. USCIS stated that employment-based cases use the Dates for Filing chart.

Dates for Filing, employment (Rest of World, includes Mexico)

  • EB-1: current.
  • EB-2: March 15, 2026.
  • EB-3 professionals and skilled workers: August 1, 2024.
  • EB-3 other workers: June 1, 2022.
  • EB-4: January 1, 2025.
  • EB-5 unreserved: current.

What to do

  • Compare your priority date (on your I-140 or I-130 I-797 notice) with the chart.
  • If your date is earlier than the bulletin date, you may prepare your adjustment of status (I-485) with your attorney.
  • Always confirm dates in the official State Department bulletin: they change every month.
