November 2026 Visa Bulletin: EB-2 and EB-3 final action dates advance for the green card

EB-2 Rest of World and Mexico: April 1, 2025 (final action). EB-3: June 15, 2024. Employment Dates for Filing hold steady.

The State Department published the November 2026 Visa Bulletin. In employment categories, EB-2 and EB-3 final action dates advance. Dates for Filing stay almost the same.

Final action dates, employment (Rest of World and Mexico)

  • EB-1: current.
  • EB-2: April 1, 2025 (October was January 1, 2025).
  • EB-3 professionals and skilled workers: June 15, 2024 (October was May 15, 2024).
  • EB-3 other workers: June 1, 2022.
  • EB-4: December 15, 2022.
  • EB-5 unreserved: current.

Dates for Filing, employment (Rest of World and Mexico)

  • EB-2: March 15, 2026. EB-3: August 1, 2024. Other workers: July 1, 2022. EB-4: January 1, 2025.
  • For November, employment-based adjustment of status (I-485) filings use the Dates for Filing chart. Confirm on the USCIS page before you file.

Family: final action dates

  • F1: Rest of World January 22, 2021; Mexico September 22, 2008.
  • F2A: Rest of World October 22, 2026; Mexico April 22, 2026.
  • F2B: Rest of World August 22, 2020; Mexico May 15, 2010.
  • F3: Rest of World October 22, 2015; Mexico July 15, 2001.
  • F4: Rest of World October 22, 2012; Mexico April 22, 2001.

What to do

  • Find your priority date on your I-797 notice (from the I-130 or I-140).
  • If your date is earlier than the date in the chart USCIS names this month, you may prepare your I-485 with your attorney.
  • Check the official chart every month: dates can move forward or back.
  • This is general information, not legal advice.
